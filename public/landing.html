How it works

Four simple steps from showing up to making a difference.

  1. 1

    Find an event

    Choose a health fair, screening, or workshop in your area.

  2. 2

    Check in

    When you arrive, point your phone camera at the event's QR code.

  3. 3

    Earn points

    Your time is recorded and you earn PACE points automatically.

  4. 4

    See the impact

    Organizers and sponsors get accurate reports they can trust.

The PACE pillars

PACE stands for four ideas that guide everything we measure.

  1. P

    Performance

    We count what matters: the hours you give, how often you show up, and the milestones you reach.

    Measured fairly
  2. A

    Accountability

    Every check-in is time-stamped and confirmed at the event, so records are honest and can be trusted.

    Verified records
  3. C

    Community

    We bring neighbors, nonprofits and sponsors together to make local health stronger.

    Stronger together
  4. E

    Engagement

    We reward people who keep coming back with points, badges and recognition they can share.

    Effort recognized
